Based on the provided hotel results, I've created a 7-day travel itinerary for Delhi, incorporating the Golden Triangle tour:

**Day 1: Arrival in Delhi**

* Arrive at Delhi International Airport
* Transfer to your pre-booked hotel by a private chauffeur-driven vehicle
* Spend the day exploring the city, visiting the hip boutiques surrounding the dramatic parkland ruins of Hauz Khas or the charming government-run state emporiums on Baba Kharak Singh Marg
* Evening: Enjoy a flavorful dinner at a local restaurant

**Day 2: Delhi**

* Visit the Red Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage Site
* Explore the Jama Masjid, one of the largest mosques in India
* Walk through the bustling streets of Chandni Chowk, one of the oldest markets in India
* Evening: Enjoy a street food tour or visit the India Gate

**Day 3: Delhi to Agra (approx. 230 km, 4 hours)**

* Drive to Agra, home to the Taj Mahal
* Visit the Taj Mahal, a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the Seven Wonders of the World
* Explore the Agra Fort, another UNESCO World Heritage Site
* Evening: Enjoy a romantic dinner at a local restaurant

**Day 4: Agra to Jaipur (approx. 240 km, 4.5 hours)**

* Drive to Jaipur, the Pink City
* Visit the Amer Palace Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage Site
* Explore the City Palace and the Jantar Mantar, an astronomical observatory
* Evening: Enjoy a traditional Rajasthani dinner and watch a cultural performance

**Day 5: Jaipur**

* Visit the Hawa Mahal, a beautiful palace with intricate architecture
* Explore the Nahargarh Fort, which offers stunning views of the city
* Visit the local markets and shop for handicrafts and souvenirs
* Evening: Enjoy a relaxing dinner at a local restaurant

**Day 6: Jaipur to Ranthambore National Park (approx. 180 km, 3.5 hours)**

* Drive to Ranthambore National Park, home to tigers and other wildlife
* Go on a safari and spot tigers, leopards, and other animals
* Evening: Enjoy a campfire and a relaxing dinner at a jungle lodge

**Day 7: Return to Delhi (approx. 400 km, 6 hours)**

* Drive back to Delhi, stopping at any attractions you missed on the way
* Spend the evening shopping for last-minute souvenirs or exploring a new neighborhood
* Depart from Delhi International Airport for your onward journey

Hotel Suggestions:

* In Delhi: Consider staying at a hotel near the city center, such as the Oberoi or the Leela
* In Agra: Stay at a hotel near the Taj Mahal, such as the Taj Palace or the Oberoi Amarvilas
* In Jaipur: Consider staying at a hotel in the city center, such as the Rambagh Palace or the Jai Mahal Palace
