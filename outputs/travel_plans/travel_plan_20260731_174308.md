## 🗓️ Itinerary
Based on your query, I've created a 5-day travel itinerary for a trip to Uttar Pradesh from Bangalore in March.

**Day 1: Arrival in Lucknow**

* Take a morning flight from Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) in Bangalore to Chaudhary Charan Singh International Airport (LKO) in Lucknow.
* Check-in to a hotel in Lucknow, such as the Hotel Clarks Avadh or the Hotel India Awadh.
* Visit the Bara Imambara, a historic monument and a major tourist attraction in Lucknow.
* Explore the local market and try some of the city's famous street food, such as kebabs and biryani.

**Day 2: Lucknow**

* Start the day with a visit to the Chhota Imambara, another historic monument in Lucknow.
* Visit the Lucknow Zoo, which is home to a wide range of animals, including tigers, lions, and monkeys.
* Explore the State Museum, which showcases the history and culture of Uttar Pradesh.
* In the evening, take a stroll along the Gomti River and enjoy the city's scenic views.

**Day 3: Ayodhya**

* Take a day trip to Ayodhya, a city located about 130 km from Lucknow.
* Visit the Ram Janmabhoomi, a temple dedicated to Lord Rama, and the Hanuman Garhi, a temple dedicated to Lord Hanuman.
* Explore the Ayodhya Fort, which offers stunning views of the city.
* Return to Lucknow in the evening and enjoy a traditional dinner at a local restaurant.

**Day 4: Varanasi**

* Take a morning flight from Lucknow to Varanasi, a city located about 300 km from Lucknow.
* Check-in to a hotel in Varanasi, such as the Hotel Hindusthan International or the Hotel India.
* Visit the Kashi Vishwanath Temple, one of the most famous temples in India.
* Take a stroll along the Ghats of Varanasi, which offer stunning views of the Ganges River.
* In the evening, enjoy a sunset cruise on the Ganges River.

**Day 5: Departure from Varanasi**

* Spend the morning shopping for souvenirs or exploring the local market.
* Visit the Benares Hindu University, which is one of the largest universities in India.
* Depart for the airport and take a flight back to Bangalore.
